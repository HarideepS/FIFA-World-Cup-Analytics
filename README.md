# ⚽ Can Data Explain FIFA World Cup Success?

### A Historical Analytics & Machine Learning Study (1930–2026)

---

## 📖 Motivation

Football is filled with widely accepted beliefs.

_"Top-ranked teams always perform better."_

_"The Ballon d'Or winner guarantees success."_

_"Host nations always have an advantage."_

_"Possession wins tournaments."_

These statements are often repeated by fans, pundits, and analysts. However, are they supported by historical evidence, or are they simply narratives reinforced over time?

This project aims to investigate these questions using historical FIFA World Cup data, statistical analysis, and machine learning techniques rather than intuition or popular opinion.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Despite the abundance of football statistics, many commonly accepted World Cup narratives remain largely untested through comprehensive historical analysis.

The objective of this project is not to predict the winner of the FIFA World Cup, but to evaluate whether popular football hypotheses have meaningful statistical relationships with tournament success.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The project aims to:

- Collect and integrate historical FIFA World Cup datasets.
- Clean and prepare data for analysis.
- Explore relationships between different football variables.
- Test widely discussed football hypotheses using statistical methods.
- Build machine learning models to identify factors associated with successful World Cup campaigns.
- Present findings through interactive visualizations and dashboards.

---

## ❓ Research Question

> **Which factors have historically shown the strongest relationship with FIFA World Cup success?**

---

## 📌 Research Hypotheses

The following hypotheses represent **testable assumptions rather than predetermined conclusions**. Their acceptance or rejection will be based solely on historical data and statistical analysis.

### Team Quality

- **H1:** Higher FIFA rankings are associated with better World Cup performance.
- **H2:** Teams with greater squad market value tend to progress further in the tournament.
- **H3:** Previous FIFA World Cup experience improves tournament performance.

### Historical Trends

- **H4:** Host nations perform better than expected.
- **H5:** European and South American teams have historically dominated the FIFA World Cup.

### Tactical Characteristics

- **H6:** Teams with higher average player age perform differently from younger squads.
- **H7:** Goals scored are a stronger indicator of tournament success than possession percentage.

### Football Ecosystem

- **H8:** Stronger domestic leagues produce stronger national teams.

### Interesting Football Narratives

- **H9:** Teams containing the reigning Ballon d'Or winner are less likely to win the FIFA World Cup than commonly believed.
- **H10:** Teams coached by managers of the same nationality perform better than teams coached by foreign managers.
